When to Visit for Romance

The best time for couples to visit is November through April during the dry season. Our December guide shows perfect weather with 8+ hours of sunshine daily. Avoid the rainy season (May-October) when some romantic activities may be canceled.

Top Romantic Resorts in Maldives

Gili Lankanfushi

This eco-luxury resort features the largest overwater villas in Maldives, each with private decks and direct lagoon access. Their "Just Us" package includes:

Soneva Jani

Famous for its retractable roof villas and waterslides into the lagoon, Soneva Jani offers:

  • Villas with private pools up to 1,400 sqm (15,000 sq ft)
  • In-villa cinema with over 500 movie selections
  • Astronomy sessions with resident astronomer
  • Personal electric buggies for getting around the island

Pro Booking Tips for Couples

  1. Book early: Reserve 6-9 months ahead for best villa selection and early bird discounts
  2. Consider seasons: November-April offers dry weather but higher prices
  3. Check inclusions: Many resorts offer free honeymoon perks when booking direct (bottle of champagne, couple's massage, etc.)
  4. Flight combos: Look for flight+resort packages from major airlines
  5. Special occasions: Mention anniversaries/birthdays - resorts often provide complimentary upgrades

Couples Resort FAQs

Overwater villas provide ultimate privacy and direct lagoon access, while beach villas offer more space and easier access to resort amenities. For first-time visitors, we recommend splitting your stay - 3 nights in each type.

Adults-only resorts like Komandoo guarantee a child-free environment, but many family-friendly resorts have adults-only areas and restaurants. If tranquility is your priority, adults-only is worth the premium.
