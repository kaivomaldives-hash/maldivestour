# 07a — Free-licence image list (Pexels / Pixabay)

These are the 14 image slots in `07-image-production-plan.csv` with no suitable photo in the media bucket.
Some files cover more than one slot, so there are fewer files than slots.
Everything else in the plan uses photos already in the bucket. I looked at each one before choosing it.

## How to pick and upload
1. Search pexels.com or pixabay.com for the term below.
2. Choose a landscape photo at least 1,600 px wide (1,080 px for square slots).
3. Skip these:
   - AI-generated images (Pixabay labels them)
   - photos with logos
   - photos where a recognisable person is the subject
4. Upload through the admin media uploader using the filename below. The uploader makes the WebP size variants.
5. Record the following in the image's title or notes, and in the caption:
   - photographer
   - source URL
   - download date
6. Caption: `Photo: <photographer> / Pexels` (or Pixabay).
7. Write the alt text from what the chosen photo actually shows.
8. Never caption a free photo as our boat, our lodge, Maamendhoo, or a catch from our trips.

## Found while reviewing the bucket
- **Lodge photos.** `gotrip/lodge/mfh-lodge-exterior.webp` shows the lounge, not the outside. There is **no exterior photo of the lodge** in the bucket. If you can take one photo yourself, make it this one.
- **Maamendhoo.** `central/gaafu-alifu-maamendhoo-maldives.webp` looks like a satellite screenshot from a map app. Map imagery usually can't be reused freely, so it isn't used in the plan.
- **Out-of-date price graphic.** `opt/legacy/images/maldives-fishing-activities.webp` is an advert showing "5 Nights 2000 USD / 8 Nights 2600 USD". Those prices don't match the current rate sheet. Don't reuse it.
- **Same photo under three names.** `gt-slayer-male`, `gt-safari-male` and `gt-fishing-guide` are the same GT photo. Use it once per page.
- **Not our boat.** `half-day-trip-deep-jigging.webp` (now used on the Emperor page) is not Emperor, so caption it generically.
- **Low resolution.** `nightfishing.webp` is only 550 px wide. Show it small, not full width.
