- **No rank-tracking, traffic or backlink tool was used.**
  - This document makes **no** claims about competitors' traffic, authority, backlinks or rankings beyond "appeared in results on this date".

## Patterns

1. **Marketplaces dominate commercial charter queries.**
   - Our advantage is the specifics of a real operator. Breadth is not an option.
   - This is why U02 and N01 are P1.
2. **Season information is contradictory online.** A sourced answer per species is a defensible gap (U12).
3. **Price transparency is rare.**
   - Observed figures range from ~USD 450 to ~USD 4,200 per day.
   - Our rate sheet lets N01 be specific.
4. **Location content for Gaafu is almost empty** for fishing travellers (U14).
5. **International comparison queries** (H, I) are held by long-standing publishers. They are lower priority, and cited sources are essential there.
